# Contributor Covenant v2.1 - Adopted by Sannel.Encoding.Manager

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our open-source community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

To create a positive environment in our open-source project:

*   **Be professional:** Maintain a professional and respectful tone in all interactions. Harassment, including any form of discrimination or intimidation, is strictly prohibited.
*   **Empower others:** Empower everyone—including other people—to participate; offer support as needed.
*   **Accept responsibility**: You own your actions, acknowledging mistakes promptly and learning from feedback without being defensive or dismissive of concerns raised by the community.
*   **Focus on what is best for the community.** Respect dissenting opinions but avoid unnecessarily divisive discussions about topics like politics, religion, etc.

## Our Responsibilities

Community moderators are responsible for clarifying what constitutes unacceptable behavior so that everyone can understand alternatives to unacceptable actions. They may also take appropriate corrective action and investigate complaints or mediation between contributors.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces including the issue tracker (GitHub issues), pull requests, commits, forum threads, social media groups dedicated to this project, code reviews, chats at conferences/online communities related to our software—and in real-world events involving members and employees when they are officially attending such as hackathons or conferences.

## Our Governance Model

This project uses a community governance model focused on collaboration rather than strict hierarchy:

*   **Maintainers:** A small group elected by the core contributors who oversee daily operations, including approving changes to this Code of Conduct, responding quickly when incidents arise within reason given time constraints for maintainers' availability (they're volunteers).
*   **Contributors:** Anyone contributing code, documentation, testing efforts etc.—with equal say regarding project direction.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community moderators are responsible for upholding the standards and expectations outlined in this Code of Conduct, investigating complaints about violations against them promptly with appropriate consequences as warranted based on severity level involved during enforcement process (from public warning → temporary ban). In cases where behavior violates applicable laws or regulations under copyright infringement claims regarding stolen material/crediting issues faced when reporting incidents outside community channels directly involving law enforcement agencies etc.—please report immediately to legal department.

## Reporting Issues

Report any behavior you've witnessed or participated in that may violate these rules via:

*   **Email:** `holtsoftware@outlook.com`
*   **GitHub Issue:** A link to a specific issue describing the incident.
